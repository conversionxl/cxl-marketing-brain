Other lifetime deal marketplaces: blank, no competitor is named in any source.

**The proof row is the finding.** Every pillar has customer quotes and no proof. The voice rules demand "Proof over claims. Name the mechanism, cite the result, show the number" (guides/voice-rules.md), and no source holds a number, a case study, or a documented result. The raw material for one exists: Jordan's 8 retainers and "pays for itself in a month" claim, and the avatar cohort's repeat buying (154 of 205 orders, customers.csv). Turning either into published proof needs the customer's permission and verified figures.
