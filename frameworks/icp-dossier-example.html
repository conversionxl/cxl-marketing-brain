The rich avatar

The founder or owner of a 1 to 5 person marketing agency or consultancy who is also the whole delivery team. Runs on client retainers, buys two or three deals a month for years, and treats a one-time price as margin, not cost.

Titles
Founder, Owner / Operator, Managing Director, Freelance Consultant
Business model
Client retainers: "8 active client retainers" (Jordan), "12-ish clients" (Marisol)
Why they buy again
"I'm not buying software, I'm buying margin."
What they really buy
Staying small without looking small. No first hire, and a client list that thinks there's a whole team.

Their words

Desires

  • Margin that grows without headcount
  • To resell what they buy, under their own logo
  • To look bigger than they are: "punching way above my weight"
  • Tools they'll "actually still use in 6 months"

Pains

  • Overhead: "what kills small agencies"
  • The first hire: "the day my margin dies"
  • Monthly bills: "one less thing draining the account"
  • Per-seat licences when every client runs under one login
