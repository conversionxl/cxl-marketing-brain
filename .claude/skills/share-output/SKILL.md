# Share an output

Brand brain pages hold customer names, quotes, revenue figures and strategy. Share them on purpose.

1. **Private by default.** Publish as a private artifact. Never make it public unless the user asks.
2. **Check what's on the page** before it leaves: customer and company names, quotes, revenue and other numbers, anything from `raw/strategy/`. Never an email address; the page should hold none already.
3. **Sharing outside the company** (a demo channel, a community, an agency): offer a redacted copy first. Replace customer names with their role and company type, remove revenue and any figure the user marks confidential, and keep the structure and the brand's own words. Publish the copy as its own artifact titled "[Brand] [output] (redacted)", or save it as a file. Never redact the original.
4. **When the org blocks sharing** (the artifact link shows "page not found" to others, or the account can't share outside the organization): say this is an admin setting, not a broken page, and offer the fallbacks: a redacted PDF or a screenshot of the page. In Claude Code, write the redacted page to `projects/marketing-brain/outputs/` and point to the browser's print-to-PDF.
5. **Say what was shared** in one line: which page, private or shared, redacted or not.
